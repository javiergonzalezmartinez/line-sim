Finite-capacity Gantt by resource (synthetic)

Synthetic and illustrative. First 14 days of the measurement window of the fictitious plant. Green = a server of a process stage is working on a lot (one lane per server); hatched amber above a stage = lots waiting in front of it (stacked, up to 4 shown). The table checks the bars: bar-hours / (servers x window) against the utilisation the simulation reports (busy). A bar runs from the start of a run to its end, so a repair in the middle of a run is inside the bar: bars match busy + repair exactly (checked), and are higher than busy alone on stages with breakdowns. Setup and preventive maintenance are not in the bars, so the last column, with them added, is larger where they exist. Not modelled: shift calendar on the lanes, vehicles, which person works where, dates.
